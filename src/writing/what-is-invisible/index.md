I am not afraid of *you* when you are strong, I am afraid of *you* when you are weak. In strength you will respond. In weakness you will react.

I am not afraid of *me* when I am strong, I am afraid of *me* when I am weak. In strength I will respond. In weakness I will react.

Deep breath, shallow breath…

I can not see your presence. I can see your body breathing.

I felt something but I did not see it. I wanted to see a feeling. This is where I got lost. I wanted to touch a feeling, instead of feeling. I wanted to describe a feeling, instead of feeling.

The moment I name a feeling, I do not feel it. It is now in my mind, not in my body. I reacted.

Unfelt.

Analysed.

Why are sound, smell, or taste able to bring me back to a place where I felt something?

Words. Language.

Triggers.

Invisible.
